Important

Dashboard ratings are research-screening scores, not exchange, company, broker or credit-rating agency ratings. VERIFIED ratings require company/business evidence with a supporting source. AUTOMATED ratings are quantitative or rule-based screening proxies and should be confirmed from exchange filings, annual reports, investor presentations and management commentary.

Tailwind

Structural sector / industry support

Tailwind measures whether the company's sector or industry is benefiting from a durable structural trend rather than only a short-term price move.

Automated Tailwind scores are based on sector and industry classification rules. Examples of positive structural drivers include:

  • Renewable-energy capacity additions and energy transition
  • Grid expansion and electrification
  • Defence localisation and domestic procurement
  • Infrastructure and capital-expenditure cycles
  • Electronics and semiconductor localisation
  • Import substitution
  • Healthcare penetration and export demand
  • Financialisation and formalisation
  • Cloud, AI and data-centre growth
  • Premiumisation and long-term consumption growth

When company-specific verified evidence is available, it can override the automated sector-level score.

Macro

Macro-economic and policy support

Macro Support measures how favourable the current broader economic, policy and investment environment is for a company's sector.

Automated Macro scores are sector-level screening heuristics. Factors can include:

  • Government infrastructure spending
  • Domestic manufacturing policies
  • Energy-transition investment
  • Credit penetration and financialisation
  • Defence procurement and localisation
  • Electrification and grid investment
  • Healthcare and export demand
  • Consumption and premiumisation
  • Commodity-cycle sensitivity
  • Technology and digital-transformation spending

Macro score is a screening aid and does not mean that every company in the sector will perform equally.

Value Migration

Business value shifting toward the company

Verified Value Migration attempts to identify whether industry value, market share, customer spending or economic profit is structurally shifting toward a company or business model.

Examples include:

  • Unorganised market shifting toward organised players
  • Imports shifting toward domestic manufacturing
  • Legacy technology shifting toward newer technology
  • Offline activity shifting toward digital platforms
  • Market share shifting from weaker players to stronger players
  • Commodity products shifting toward higher-value specialty products
  • Traditional energy shifting toward renewable / clean energy
  • Customer spending shifting toward a new distribution model

A VERIFIED Value Migration score requires business/company evidence.

When verified evidence is not available, the dashboard may display an AUTOMATED screening proxy based on short-term price momentum and turnover. That proxy is not treated as verified business Value Migration.

Current automated screening proxy:

60% short-term price momentum + 40% turnover percentile

Future Growth

Growth potential screening score

Future Growth attempts to measure the company's ability to grow revenue and earnings in coming periods.

Automated scoring currently uses available financial-data proxies such as:

  • Revenue growth
  • Earnings growth
  • Quarterly earnings growth
  • Available forward versus trailing valuation data

A verified company-level Future Growth assessment can additionally consider:

  • Order book
  • Capacity expansion
  • Management guidance
  • New products
  • New customers
  • New geographies
  • Market-share gains
  • Industry growth outlook

Fundamental

Financial quality screening score

Fundamental Quality measures the financial strength and quality of the business using available quantitative financial indicators.

Automated scoring can include:

  • Return on Equity (ROE)
  • Profit margin
  • Operating margin
  • Debt-to-equity
  • Current ratio
  • Free cash flow
  • Operating cash flow

A higher score generally indicates stronger profitability, balance-sheet quality, liquidity and cash-generation characteristics based on the available data.

Automated data can occasionally be unavailable or rate-limited. In those cases, the score should be treated cautiously and verified against company filings.

CAPEX

Capital expenditure / expansion screening score

CAPEX score attempts to identify whether the company is actively investing in productive capacity, plant, equipment or expansion.

The automated screening proxy compares available capital expenditure with operating cash flow.

Automated CAPEX proxy:

CAPEX / Operating Cash Flow

≥ 0.50 → 90
≥ 0.30 → 80
≥ 0.15 → 70
≥ 0.05 → 60
> 0 → 50

A verified CAPEX assessment can additionally use:

  • Company-announced CAPEX plans
  • Capacity additions
  • New plant commissioning
  • Expansion timeline
  • Expected revenue contribution
  • Management commentary
  • NSE / company filings

Evidence Status

Each rating popup can show an evidence status.

VERIFIED

Company/business evidence is available with a supporting reason and source. Verified evidence can override an automated screening proxy.

AUTOMATED

Score is generated using the dashboard's quantitative or rule-based screening methodology. It is not a manually verified company-specific conclusion.

PENDING

Reliable data or evidence is currently insufficient to calculate or verify the rating.
